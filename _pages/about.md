Welcome to the official website of the ZOC Ocular Epidemiology Consortium (ZOC-OEC). We integrate three major ophthalmic cohorts to advance global ocular epidemiology research. 

By combining traditional epidemiological methods with advanced AI prediction models, including Transformer architectures and multimodal data fusion (proteomics and imaging), we aim to provide cutting-edge insights into the mechanisms and prevention of blinding eye diseases.

## Our Core Cohorts

### 1. The ZAP Cohort (Zhongshan Angle Closure Prevention Trial)
A pioneering long-term prospective study. Our current research focuses on the landmark 18-year follow-up of the ZAP cohort, exploring new longitudinal research questions and utilizing advanced anterior segment imaging, such as CASIA2 measurements, to understand the long-term progression of angle closure.

### 2. High Myopia Cohort
Dedicated to the clinical research of high myopia and its associated complications. A major focus of our work is investigating the mechanism and developing prediction models for the evolution of High Myopic Optic Neuropathy (HMON), specifically driven by choroidal ischemia and Bruch's membrane injury.
