Our multidisciplinary team integrates expertise in clinical ophthalmology, epidemiology, and artificial intelligence to tackle blinding eye diseases.

## Principal Investigator (PI)

  <div>
    <h3 style="margin-top: 0; margin-bottom: 5px;">Prof. Wei Wang (M.D., Ph.D.)</h3>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 10px;"><strong>Principal Investigator / Associate Chief Physician<br>State Key Laboratory of Ophthalmology, Zhongshan Ophthalmic Center, Sun Yat-sen University</strong></p>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 10px;">Prof. Wei Wang is a highly acclaimed ophthalmologist, Principal Investigator (PI) at the State Key Laboratory of Ophthalmology, and Director of the Development Planning Office at Zhongshan Ophthalmic Center. He also served as a visiting scholar at the National Ophthalmic Center in Australia.</p>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 10px;">Recognized globally for his outstanding contributions, Prof. Wang has been named one of the <strong>"Asia-Pacific 100 Most Influential Ophthalmologists"</strong> and listed among the <strong>"World's Top 2% Scientists"</strong> by Stanford University for five consecutive years. According to Expertscape, he ranks in the top 0.1% of glaucoma experts globally, specifically within the top 20 worldwide for angle-closure and neovascular glaucoma.</p>
    <ul style="margin-bottom: 10px;">
      <li><strong>Glaucoma Prevention:</strong> Led the extended Zhongshan Angle-Closure Prevention (ZAP-Ex) trial, establishing population risk stratification standards and identifying abnormal uveal hemodynamics.</li>
      <li><strong>High Myopia Cohort:</strong> Established the world's longest-running and most comprehensive high myopia evolution cohort, defining international standards that were incorporated into the International Myopia Institute (IMI) 2025 White Paper.</li>
      <li><strong>Diabetic Eye Disease:</strong> Innovated a preventive research framework by revealing key metabolic-proteomic signatures for diabetic retinopathy.</li>
      <li><strong>"Eye-Heart-Brain-Kidney Axis":</strong> Proposed a groundbreaking cross-disciplinary model utilizing large-scale cohort data and advanced algorithms, highlighted as a Hot Topic by the American Academy of Ophthalmology (AAO).</li>
    </ul>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 0;"><strong>Academic Excellence:</strong> He has published <strong>310 SCI-indexed papers</strong> (166 as first/corresponding author in top-tier journals like <em>Ophthalmology</em>, <em>Nature Communications</em>, and <em>JAMA Ophthalmology</em>), with over 20,000 citations and an H-index of 69.</p>
