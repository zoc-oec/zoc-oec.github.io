Our multidisciplinary team integrates expertise in clinical ophthalmology, epidemiology, and artificial intelligence to tackle blinding eye diseases.

## Principal Investigator (PI)
<img src="/images/wangwei.png" width="140" align="left" style="margin-right: 20px; margin-bottom: 20px; border-radius: 8px;">
### Prof. Wei Wang (M.D., Ph.D.)
**Principal Investigator / Associate Chief Physician**  
**State Key Laboratory of Ophthalmology, Zhongshan Ophthalmic Center, Sun Yat-sen University**

Prof. Wei Wang is a highly acclaimed ophthalmologist, Principal Investigator (PI) at the State Key Laboratory of Ophthalmology, and Director of the Development Planning Office at Zhongshan Ophthalmic Center[cite: 1]. He also served as a visiting scholar at the National Ophthalmic Center in Australia[cite: 1]. 

Recognized globally for his outstanding contributions, Prof. Wang has been named one of the **"Asia-Pacific 100 Most Influential Ophthalmologists"** and listed among the **"World's Top 2% Scientists"** by Stanford University for five consecutive years[cite: 1]. According to Expertscape, he ranks in the top 0.1% of glaucoma experts globally, specifically within the top 20 worldwide for angle-closure and neovascular glaucoma[cite: 1].

**Key Research Contributions & Scientific Leadership:**
* **Glaucoma Prevention:** Led the extended Zhongshan Angle-Closure Prevention (ZAP-Ex) trial, establishing population risk stratification standards and identifying abnormal uveal hemodynamics[cite: 1].
* **High Myopia Cohort:** Established the world's longest-running and most comprehensive high myopia evolution cohort, defining international standards for morphological and neurological damage assessment, which were incorporated into the International Myopia Institute (IMI) 2025 White Paper[cite: 1].
* **Diabetic Eye Disease:** Innovated a preventive research framework by revealing key molecular (metabolic-proteomic) signatures for diabetic retinopathy, pioneering new modes for precise screening[cite: 1].
* **"Eye-Heart-Brain-Kidney Axis":** Proposed a groundbreaking cross-disciplinary model utilizing large-scale cohort data and advanced algorithms to uncover shared molecular anomalies, a study highlighted as a Hot Topic by the American Academy of Ophthalmology (AAO)[cite: 1].
* **Global Eye Health:** Contributed to the strategic goals for universal eye health, which were integrated into the WHO's "Eye Care 2030 Targets"[cite: 1].

**Academic Excellence:**
* He has published **310 SCI-indexed papers**, including 166 as first or corresponding author in top-tier journals such as *Ophthalmology*, *Nature Communications* (Nature sub-journals), and *JAMA Ophthalmology*[cite: 1].
* His research has garnered over **20,000 citations** with an impressive **H-index of 69** (Google Scholar)[cite: 1]. 
* He serves on the editorial board of the *Asia-Pacific Journal of Ophthalmology* and acts as a reviewer for leading journals including *The Lancet Global Health* and *Ophthalmology*[cite: 1].
