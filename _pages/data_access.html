--- layout: archive title: "Data Access & Collaboration" permalink: /data-access/ author_profile: true --- The ZOC Ocular Epidemiology Consortium (ZOC-OEC) is committed to open science and accelerating discoveries in blinding eye diseases. We welcome collaboration proposals from researchers and institutions worldwide. ## Data Sharing Policy De-identified clinical, imaging, and multi-omics data from our core cohorts (**ZAP**, **GDES**, and **ZHMC**) may be made available to qualified researchers. Access is granted subject to scientific review of the proposed research, ethical approval (IRB), and formal data-sharing agreements in accordance with Sun Yat-sen University guidelines. ## Application Procedure
  1. Prepare your proposal: Outline your research question, methodology, and the specific variables required from our cohorts.
  2. Submit request: Send a formal proposal and a copy of your local IRB/Ethics approval to our data access committee.
  3. Review process: Proposals are reviewed monthly for scientific merit, feasibility, and overlap with our ongoing internal projects.
  4. Agreement: Upon approval, a formal Data Transfer Agreement (DTA) must be signed by both institutions before any data is securely transferred.
