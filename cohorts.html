Our Cohorts

Three complementary longitudinal cohorts covering the major causes of preventable blindness in Asia: angle-closure glaucoma, diabetic eye disease, and high myopia.

ZAP

Zhongshan Angle-Closure Prevention Trial

The ZAP trial is a landmark randomized controlled trial evaluating whether laser peripheral iridotomy (LPI) prevents angle closure and glaucoma in Chinese adults with narrow drainage angles (primary angle-closure suspect, PACS). Initiated at Zhongshan Ophthalmic Center in Guangzhou, ZAP has followed over 2,000 eyes across ~1,000 participants for 14 years, generating the longest follow-up data on angle closure prevention in the literature.

The 2019 Lancet publication of the primary trial results (He M et al., Lancet 2019) demonstrated that LPI significantly reduces angle closure progression. Subsequent analyses have characterized the biometric risk factors, anatomical predictors, and long-term durability of the intervention through 14-year follow-up (Yuan Y et al., Ophthalmology 2023; JAMA Ophthalmol 2024).

GDES

Guangzhou Diabetic Eye Study

GDES is a prospective longitudinal cohort of adults with type 2 diabetes mellitus recruited from the outpatient endocrinology service at Zhongshan Ophthalmic Center. The study integrates ophthalmic imaging (OCT, OCTA, fundus photography) with systemic phenotyping, plasma metabolomics, proteomics, and genomics to characterize the ocular and systemic consequences of diabetes and discover novel biomarkers.

GDES has produced landmark multi-omics analyses linking retinal phenotypes to metabolomic signatures of systemic disease (Nat Commun 2023, 2025), predictive models for diabetic retinopathy progression (Ophthalmology 2024), and OCTA-based biomarkers of renal and cardiovascular dysfunction. The cohort's 4-year longitudinal follow-up enables both incidence and progression analyses.

ZHMC

ZOC–BHVI High Myopia Cohort

The ZOC–BHVI High Myopia Cohort (ZHMC) is a prospective longitudinal study of adults and children with high myopia (spherical equivalent ≤ −6.00 D) established in collaboration with the Brien Holden Vision Institute, Sydney. The cohort characterizes 10-year trajectories of axial elongation, macular structural change, and functional loss, and evaluates emerging interventions including repeated low-level red-light (RLRL) therapy and orthokeratology.

ZHMC has defined axial length trajectories in Chinese high myopes (JAMA Ophthalmol 2024), demonstrated the efficacy of RLRL combined with orthokeratology (Ophthalmology 2024), established normative RNFL databases for detecting myopic glaucoma (Ophthalmology 2023), and built machine learning models to predict myopic maculopathy risk over a decade.
