ZAP

Zhongshan Angle-Closure Prevention Trial

ZAP — the Zhongshan Angle-Closure Prevention Trial — is a within-person randomized trial testing whether laser peripheral iridotomy prevents angle-closure progression in primary angle-closure suspects. Long follow-up and deep AS-OCT phenotyping make it the consortium's clearest platform for prevention and progression research.

Design strengthWithin-person randomization: one treated eye, one observed eye.
Imaging depthGonioscopy, AS-OCT, IOP, visual fields, and anterior-segment biometry.
Scientific usePrevention evidence, risk prediction, and natural-history modelling.

GDES

Guangzhou Diabetic Eye Study

GDES — the Guangzhou Diabetic Eye Study — is a deeply phenotyped type-2-diabetes eye cohort that links multimodal retinal imaging with plasma proteomics and metabolomics to systemic outcomes, turning the retina into a systemic-biomarker platform.

Multi-modal imagingFundus photography, OCT, SS-OCTA, and longitudinal retinal phenotypes.
Multi-omics layerPlasma proteomics, metabolomics, and genetic resources.
Systemic linksCardiometabolic, renal, mortality, and diabetic complication outcomes.

ZHMC

Zhongshan High Myopia Cohort

ZHMC — the Zhongshan High Myopia Cohort — is a long-running clinical registry and prospective platform for high-myopia progression in children and adults. Together with its sub-cohorts it now follows more than 7,000 participants, combining deep OCT/OCTA imaging with AI risk modelling and myopia-control intervention trials.

High-myopia registryLongitudinal records for children and adults with high myopia.
OCT/OCTA biomarkersMacular, choroidal, vascular, and peripapillary imaging measures.
Intervention evidenceMyopia-control trials and real-world treatment studies.
