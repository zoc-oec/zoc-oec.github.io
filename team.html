WW
Principal Investigator

Prof. Wei Wang

Professor & Associate Director, Zhongshan Ophthalmic Center
Sun Yat-sen University · Guangzhou, China

Professor Wei Wang leads the ZOC-OEC programme, directing three major longitudinal cohorts spanning glaucoma, diabetic eye disease, and high myopia. His research integrates population-based epidemiology, AI-assisted imaging analysis, and multi-omics to build precision risk models for ocular diseases. He has published over 310 peer-reviewed papers (citations >20,000, H-index 69) in journals including Lancet, JAMA, Nature Communications, and Ophthalmology.

Research Focus Areas

🔬

Ocular Epidemiology

Large-scale longitudinal cohort studies examining incidence, progression, and risk factors of glaucoma, diabetic retinopathy, and myopic maculopathy in Chinese populations.

🤖

AI & Imaging

Deep learning models for automated grading, retinal phenotyping, and risk prediction using fundus photography, OCT, and OCTA datasets at scale.

🧬

Multi-omics Integration

Integration of metabolomics, proteomics, and genomics with retinal imaging to discover systemic biomarkers and mechanisms underlying ocular disease.

💊

Clinical Trials

Randomized controlled trials evaluating novel interventions including laser iridotomy for angle closure and red-light therapy for myopia control.
