Workflow

Application Process

1

Submit a Research Proposal

Send a concise proposal describing the scientific question, requested cohort variables, analysis plan, and expected output.

2

Complete Ethics Review

Applicants provide home-institution IRB or equivalent approval for the proposed secondary analysis.

3

Sign a Data Transfer Agreement

The DTA defines the approved dataset, analysis scope, publication expectations, and data security obligations.

4

Receive De-identified Data

Approved datasets are prepared by the Will-Lab team and transferred through secure institutional channels.

Eligibility Criteria

  • EligibleAcademic or non-profit institution
  • EligiblePI-level investigator or research team
  • RequiredAligned with Will-Lab cohort strengths
  • RequiredSecure data environment and oversight

Data Use Boundaries

  • Not allowedRe-identification or third-party sharing
  • ApprovalCommercial or industry-sponsored use
  • RequiredAcknowledge Will-Lab and the cohort
  • RequiredPre-submission manuscript review
