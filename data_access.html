Application Process

1

Submit a Research Proposal

Prepare a 1â2 page proposal describing your scientific question, target dataset(s), analysis plan, and expected outcomes. Proposals are reviewed by the ZOC-OEC Data Access Committee on a rolling basis. Contact wangwei@gzzoc.com to obtain the proposal template and discuss feasibility.

2

Ethics & IRB Approval

Applicants must obtain IRB or equivalent ethics committee approval from their home institution covering the proposed secondary analysis. ZOC-OEC will provide documentation of the original ethics approvals (ZOC Ethics Committee, Sun Yat-sen University) to support your local application. All data use must comply with Chinese data protection regulations.

3

Sign a Data Transfer Agreement

A formal Data Transfer Agreement (DTA) must be executed between your institution and Sun Yat-sen University / Zhongshan Ophthalmic Center. The DTA specifies the scope of data provided, permitted analyses, publication requirements, and data security obligations. Our team will provide a standard DTA template for institutional review.

4

Receive Data & Begin Analysis

Upon approval and DTA execution, de-identified datasets are transferred via secure encrypted channels. A ZOC-OEC team member will serve as your data liaison. We expect collaborative authorship on resulting publications. Annual progress reports are required for multi-year data access agreements.
